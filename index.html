Five on five pixel hoops for up to four players. Anyone can press start to jump in, even mid-game.

Controls
PAD
Stick or d-pad to run. ✕ to jump — hold, release at the top to shoot. □ to reach or steal. ○ or △ to pass (or call for the ball). Options to join or pause.
KEYS 1
WASD run, Space shoot, E grab, F pass, Q join.
KEYS 2
Arrows run, / shoot, . grab, , pass, Enter or right Shift join.
TOUCH
Drag anywhere on the left half to run. Hold SHOOT, release at the top of the jump. GRAB steals, PASS passes. ☰ pauses.
MOUSE
Runs toward the cursor. Hold left to shoot, right to grab, middle click to pass, M to join.
PASS
Goes to the nearest teammate in the direction you're pushing, or facing if you're standing still.
TEAMS
Each side has two tall players: a quick sharpshooter and a slower rim protector.
LOBBY
Grab or click the team tag to switch teams. Start again to start the game. Esc pauses.
